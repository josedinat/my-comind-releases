# My-CoMind for Windows

Downloads for the My-CoMind desktop capture agent.

**[Download the latest installer](../../releases/latest)**

## What it is

My-CoMind captures meetings from your computer and turns them into memories in
your account, without you having to open a browser and start a recording.

It works with Teams, Meet, Zoom or anything else because it integrates with none
of them. It only knows that audio is going out of your speakers and coming in
your microphone.

## What it records

It never starts on its own. Every recording begins with you choosing to start it.

While one is running there is a window on your screen the whole time — a blinking
red dot, how long it has recorded, and buttons to pause or stop. You never have
to wonder whether it is still going.

You choose whether to capture the whole meeting — your microphone plus the audio
coming out of your speakers, which includes everyone else's voices — or only
your own microphone. At the end you are asked whether the audio itself may be
kept; if you say no, only the written summary is stored.

Recordings stay on your computer only until they have been sent, then they are
deleted.

Please make sure everyone on a call knows it is being recorded before you capture
it. Other people's voices are their information, not just yours.

## Installing

The installer is per-user: it needs no administrator rights and raises no UAC
prompt.

It is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn you the first time.
Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Every release publishes a
`SHA256SUMS.txt` you can check the download against.

## Pairing

After installing, open My-CoMind in your browser and go to
**Security Settings → Paired Devices → Pair a new device**. Enter the code in the
desktop app.

A paired computer can send captures and nothing else — it cannot read your
memories or change your settings. You can remove it at any time from the same
screen, and changing your account password removes it automatically.
